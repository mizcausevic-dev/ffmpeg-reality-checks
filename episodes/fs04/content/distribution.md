### Description

Speed up a clip, loop it, keep every frame: 900 frames, but only 450 distinct timestamps. Five ways an FFmpeg frame rate change lies, and the command that does not.

Measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1 with synthetic clips where every frame carries its own code, read two ways: ffprobe timestamps and a decode.

Variable to constant frame rate: 93 frames in, 150 out with 57 duplicates, and -fps_mode passthrough keeps 93. -r 10 after -i gives 22 frames and 2.2 s, the fps filter gives 20, and -r 10 before -i plays 6 s. fps=1 keeps frames 12, 37, 62, the first at 0.48 s. setpts=0.5*PTS gives 5.07 s of picture and 10.0 s of audio, atempo=2 fixes the audio. -stream_loop 2 with audio 3.9 s keeps 355 frames.

The recipe: ffmpeg -stream_loop 2 -i in.mkv -vf setpts=0.5*PTS,fps=60 -af atempo=2 out.mkv gives 900 frames, 900 distinct timestamps, 15 s of picture and sound, 440 Hz.
