### Description

One FFmpeg command, exit status 0, and the MP4 still has three problems a player can trip over.

Pixel format, odd size, index position, plus the HEVC label, all read from the file and not from a player.

RGB PNG frames with no -pix_fmt: High 4:4:4 Predictive, yuv444p. scale=-1:333 on 1000x562 gives 593x333 and libx264 refuses it (width not divisible by 2). 4:4:4 hid that. scale=-2:334 gives 594x334. Without -movflags +faststart the index (moov) starts at byte 917,559 of 927,238; a client that cannot seek is served the whole file against under a quarter of the faststart file. -c copy -tag:v hvc1 changes 0 of 50 frames. Fresh-frame check: High, yuv420p, 594x334, moov before mdat.

Full version: Why an FFmpeg MP4 Will Not Play: pix_fmt, Odd Size, faststart, hvc1

Measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1 with synthetic frames and two independent readers. Not tested: any player or browser, other encoders, real footage, other builds. Code and tests: github.com/mizcausevic-dev/ffmpeg-reality-checks
