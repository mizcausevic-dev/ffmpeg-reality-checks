# FF94 distribution kit: FFmpeg Resampling: 100 Pieces Joined Come Out 25 Samples Longer Than the Whole

All numbers below come from the synthetic fixture in `episodes/ff94/fixture/` (experiment.mjs, run.mjs) and its tests, measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1; also run on 8.1.2, where the tests pass (3 of 3 runs; exact numbers are asserted on 6.1.1 only, relations on 8.1.2).

### Description

You convert audio from 48000 Hz to 44100 Hz and the length is off by a sample, a short clip is empty, or a file made of many pieces is longer than the whole. How many samples should come out, and what does each method do? We resample a tone four ways and count with two readers.

Input (Node-made, piped to ffmpeg as s16le): a 200 Hz sine, amplitude 16000. Directions: 48000 to 44100, 44100 to 48000, 96000 to 44100. Input lengths: exactly one second, 2048, 1000, 1001 and 20 samples. Methods: aresample=RO, -ar RO on the output, aresample=resampler=soxr:osr=RO, and asetrate=RO. 60 outputs.

Readers: the raw bytes ffmpeg writes to stdout, counted in Node; the same command written to a wav, with the RIFF data chunk size read in Node and ffprobe duration_ts. They agree on all 60 outputs. A second check compares the output with the ideal sine at the output instants, and the tone offset in microseconds.

Control: exactly one second in gives exactly one second out for aresample, -ar and soxr in all three directions (44100, 48000 and 44100 samples), the tone is within 1 LSB of the ideal sine in the interior (a one sample shift would be 456, 419 and 456 LSB) and within 10 microseconds at both ends. The first sample can be off by up to 79 LSB.

Odd lengths: 1000 samples at 48000 give 919 at 44100 (ideal 918.75), 1001 give 920 (919.669), and 2048 give 1881 (ideal 1881.6, rounded down); soxr gives 919, 920 and 1882. 44100 to 48000: 1000 gives 1089 (soxr 1088, ideal 1088.435). The count is within one sample of the ideal but the direction depends on engine and length. -ar gives the same samples (same md5) as aresample in all 15 cases.

Short input: 20 samples give 0 samples with aresample and -ar (empty wav, exit code 0) in all three directions, and 18, 22 and 9 with soxr (ideals 18.375, 21.769 and 9.188).

asetrate does not resample: 48000 samples stay 48000 samples, now 1.0884 s long at 44100, and the 200 Hz tone becomes 183.75 Hz (217.69 Hz and 91.875 Hz for the other directions).

100 pieces of 1000 samples, 48000 to 44100, each resampled alone and joined with the concat demuxer: every piece gives 919, the join has 91900 samples, the whole signal resampled at once has 91875, so the join is 25 samples (0.567 ms) long, and the tone at the end is 561 microseconds late. Pieces of 1001 give 920 each, a drift of 33 samples (0.748 ms). soxr gives the same drift for 1000. Pieces of 4800 samples (exactly 4410 out) drift by 0.

The 12 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1.
