### Description

You speed a video up 2x with -vf setpts=0.5*PTS and the audio is out of sync. Why? setpts changes the picture only. We measure how long each stream runs, which frames survive, and what happens to the pitch.

Source (lavfi, lossless): 10 s at 30 fps, 300 frames of 64x64 gray, each with a unique code (luma carries N mod 256, chroma carries the high part), and a 10 s mono sine at 440 Hz, 44100 Hz. Control with no speed change: picture 10 s, audio 10 s, container 10 s, 300 frames, 440 Hz.

Readers: ffprobe packets (last timestamp plus duration), and a decode (frame md5, decoded pixel codes, audio sample count). Pitch by a Goertzel scan and by zero crossings. They agree on every output.

-vf setpts=0.5*PTS alone: the picture lasts 5.07 s, the audio is still 10.0 s and the container says 10.0 s. The audio plays on for almost 5 s after the picture ends. At 2.5 s into the output the picture shows source second 4.9.

At the default 30 fps output only 152 of the 300 frames survive: source frames 0, 1, 2, 3, then every second frame (5, 7, 9). With -fps_mode passthrough all 300 are kept, in 5.0 s. setpts=0.5*PTS,fps=30 keeps 150.

Adding -af atempo=2: audio 5.0 s, picture 5.07 s, container 5.07 s, pitch still 440 Hz. -af asetrate=88200,aresample=44100 also gives 5.0 s of audio, but the pitch moves to 880 Hz.

The ten tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
