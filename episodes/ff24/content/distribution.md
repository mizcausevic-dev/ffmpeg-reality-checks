### Description

You strip a file with -map_metadata -1 so it carries no personal tags. Are the chapters gone too? We make a synthetic video with a global title and two chapters, and strip it five ways, with one untouched copy as a control.

Source: a 4 second synthetic MP4 (lavfi testsrc2 video and a 440 Hz tone) with a made-up global title ("Secret Global Title") and two made-up chapters at 0 s and 2 s ("Private Chapter One", "Private Chapter Two"), written from an ffmetadata file.

Readers: ffprobe (chapters), ffmpeg's own ffmetadata export (its [CHAPTER] blocks), and a raw byte scan of the file for the title strings.

-map_metadata -1 -c copy: every title string is gone (global and both chapters), but both chapters are still there, at 0 s and 2 s, with blank titles. A re-encode with -map_metadata -1 leaves the same two chapters, and Matroska output keeps two chapters with no titles.

-map_chapters -1: the chapters are gone and the global title stays. Both flags together leave nothing.

Both chapter readers agree on the count for every output. The seven tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
