### Description

You pad the end of a clip with tpad to hold the last frame while the music rings out, and the screen goes black. What does tpad actually add, what does stop_mode=clone change, and what else catches people? We pad a counter clip and read back every added frame.

Bench (lavfi, no real footage): a 50 frame clip, 64x48, 25 fps, yuv420p, every frame a flat card with luma 60 plus 3 times its number, chroma 100 and 150, so a decoded frame names itself and no card looks like black. The filter is tpad with stop, stop_duration, stop_mode, start, start_duration, start_mode and color; for sound, apad.

Readers for the picture: Node on the raw decoded planes (luma, cb and cr of every frame, and whether each plane is flat), and ffmpeg framemd5 (every output hash looked up in the hashes of the 50 source frames and in the hash of a black, white and red frame made by the color source, no tpad). They agree on every case. For pixel formats, signalstats is the second reader. For sound, raw samples in Node and silencedetect; for the file, ffprobe. Controls: 25 black frames and 25 copies of the last card, both added with concat and no tpad: both readers return exactly that.

What the default adds: stop_duration=1 adds 25 frames of black, luma 16, cb 128, cr 128, the same frame the color source makes. The default stop_mode is add: it paints a color. color=white gives 235/128/128, color=red 81/90/240.

What clone does: stop_mode=clone with stop_duration=1 (or stop=10) adds copies of card 49; their hashes equal the real last frame, and a color given with clone is ignored. start_mode=clone repeats card 0 at the front, start and start_duration mirror stop.

Two traps: stop_mode=clone with no stop and no stop_duration adds nothing (50 frames in, 50 out). And stop=10 together with stop_duration=1 adds 25 frames, not 10: the duration wins.

Rounding: stop_duration is rounded to whole frames, halves up on this build: 0.01 s adds 0 frames, 0.02 s adds 1, 0.05 s adds 1, 0.06 s adds 2, 0.1 s adds 3, 0.3 s adds 8, 0.5 s adds 13 at 25 fps. One second adds 24, 25 and 30 frames at 24, 25 and 30 fps.

Black depends on the format: 16/128/128 in yuv420p, 0 in full-range yuvj420p, 0/0/0 in rgb24 (luma 16 once converted), 16 in gray.

Sound: apad adds silence, not a held tone: 8000 samples, every one 0, with pad_dur, pad_len, whole_len or whole_dur alike; silencedetect puts the start at 2 s. In a muxed file, tpad alone gives 3 s of video and 2 s of audio; -shortest drops the freeze (75 frames become 50); apad on the audio keeps all 3 s.

stop=-1 never ends: guarded with -frames:v 120 it gives 120 frames; unguarded it was still running when killed after 3 s.

The 15 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1.
