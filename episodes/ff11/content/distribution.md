### Description

x264 has a lossless mode, -crf 0. Feed it RGB frames and you expect them back bit for bit. Do you get them? We encode a synthetic test pattern four ways, decode every result and compare with the source.

The source: 25 frames, 320x180, planar RGB. We compare two independent ways: an md5 of every decoded frame, and the PSNR filter (inf means no difference at all).

Controls: FFV1 and libx264rgb -crf 0 both give 25 of 25 identical frames and PSNR inf.

Plain libx264 -crf 0: given RGB, it stores yuv444p. Decoded back to RGB, 0 of 25 frames are identical and PSNR is 54.6 dB. Against the RGB source, it is not lossless.

Compared in its own format (yuv444p), the same output is 25 of 25 identical. So -crf 0 is lossless for what the encoder was given. The difference comes in the conversion between RGB and YUV.

Add -pix_fmt yuv420p and PSNR against the RGB source drops to 24.0 dB on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 22.8 dB on 8.1.2. Still exact in yuv420p, but RGB cannot come back.

If you need RGB back exactly, use libx264rgb or a lossless codec such as FFV1.
