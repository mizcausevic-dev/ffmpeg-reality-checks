# FF62 distribution kit: FFmpeg zoompan Jitter: 1.2 px Wobble, Scale 8x Leaves 0.18

### Description

A slow centred zoom with zoompan should be smooth, and the centre of the picture should not move at all. It shakes. We draw a test card with a fine grid and known markers, render the common recipe, and measure where the markers really are in every decoded frame.

Fixture (no footage, no network): a 960x540 card from lavfi, grey ground, a 1 pixel grid every 8 pixels, and six white 9x9 markers in three pairs, one frame. zoompan renders 120 frames of z='min(zoom+STEP,1.5)' centred on the picture: STEP 0.0005, 0.0015 (the recipe everyone copies) and 0.004. Five ways of feeding it: as is (yuv420p), format=yuv444p first, and scale 2x, 4x, 8x first. That is 15 runs.

Readers: (1) Node finds the centre of the first marker of each pair as the weighted centroid of its pixels; (2) Node finds the edges of the second marker, 32 pixels away, by half level crossings with linear interpolation. The two agree within 0.22 px frame by frame in all 15 runs. Controls: a known sub pixel pan made without zoompan (0.25 px per frame, both readers within 0.12 px), a known jitter of exactly one pixel made with crop (reported as 1.00 on every step), and zoompan with no zoom and with a fixed zoom of 1.25 (step error 0.00).

Findings on ffmpeg 6.1.1: (1) As is, at the recipe speed, the markers miss the ideal step by 1.23 px rms (3 markers x 119 steps); 346 of 357 steps are off by more than half a pixel, and the markers stray up to 3.5 px from where a perfect zoom puts them. The centre marker alone, which a centred zoom should never move, misses by 1.22 px rms. Faster is worse: 0.75, 1.23 and 1.61 px at steps of 0.0005, 0.0015 and 0.004. (2) The cause: the lens is a whole number rectangle. The crop origin is rounded down, in yuv420p to an even number, and the width is rounded down. At the recipe speed the ideal origin glides 0.72, 1.44, 2.15, 2.86 while the real one is 0, 0, 2, 2. That integer lens model reproduces every measured track of all 15 runs to within 0.16 px; the wrong alignment is off by 1.0 to 1.3 px rms. (3) What helps, at the recipe speed: format=yuv444p first 0.69 px rms (309 of 357 steps over half a pixel), scale 2x first 0.72 (307), 4x 0.32 (24), 8x 0.18 (0). Scaling 2x first buys nothing over the one line format=yuv444p. 8x never reaches zero (0.16 to 0.18 at all three speeds) and starts zoompan from 7680x4320, 64 times the pixels.

The 16 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1.

Limits: ffmpeg 6.1.1 on Linux, a synthetic 960x540 card, a centred zoom, the common z expression, the default bicubic scaler, 25 fps, 120 frames. The yuv420p even alignment and the rounding of the crop are build specific parts and are the most likely to differ on other builds. Not tested: other builds, real photos, pans, other scalers, other output sizes, the encoder.
