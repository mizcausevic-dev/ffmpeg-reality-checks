### Description

atempo changes speed and keeps the pitch. Which speeds does it accept, and what does it really do to the sound? We build two synthetic sources at 48000 Hz mono, 4 s each (192000 samples): a 1000 Hz sine at 0.9 of full scale, and a click train with one single-sample click (29491, bytes 33 73) every 4800 samples, 40 clicks. We run atempo on both and measure. Every command carries a hard -t 30.

Range, on ffmpeg 6.1.1: atempo accepts 0.5 to 100. 0.25, 0.4, 100.5 and 200 are refused with "Value 0.400000 for parameter 'tempo' out of range [0.5 - 100]" and no output. The nine values tried inside the range (0.5, 0.8, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 4, 10, 100) all run. Chains go below 0.5: atempo=0.5,atempo=0.8 (speed 0.4) and atempo=0.5,atempo=0.5 (0.25) both run.

Length: close, not exact. 0.5 should give 384000 samples and gives 382896 (1104 short). 2 gives 95952 of 96000. 4 gives 47856 of 48000. 10 gives 18432 of 19200. 100 gives 1024 of 1920. 1.5 gives 128016 (16 over), 2.5 gives exactly 76800. Chain 0.5 then 0.8 gives 478464 of 480000; chain 100 then 2 (speed 200) gives 0 samples, an empty file with no error. Readers: the sample count on a raw pipe and ffprobe duration_ts agree on every non-empty output.

Pitch holds: zero crossings read 999.87 Hz at 0.5, 999.46 at 2, 998.77 at 4 and 994.9 at 10 (939.33 at 100, where only 1024 samples are left). A Goertzel filter at 1000 Hz reads 1842 on every atempo output from 0.5 to 10. The known-positive control, speed by resampling (asetrate then aresample), moves the tone to 500, 2000.02 and 4000.1 Hz at 0.5, 2 and 4, and Goertzel at 1000 Hz reads 0 there.

Timing does not hold. 40 clicks in; events out (samples at or above 0.1 of full scale, merged within 10 ms): 122 at 0.5, 70 at 0.8, 55 at 1, 40 at 1.5, 31 at 2, 26 at 2.5, 13 at 4, 5 at 10, 1 at 100. Chain 0.5 then 0.8: 176. atempo=1 is not a bypass: 191952 samples, a different md5 and 55 events. At 0.5 an ideal output has clicks at samples 0, 9600 and 19200 only; this one has events at samples 0, 6848, 8128 and 9408 with peaks 29490, 6521, 29486 and 29478. The resampling control keeps exactly 40 events, and so does anull. A second reader, ffmpeg silencedetect, counts the same events as the Node scan on every single value.

The 15 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1. Exact counts are asserted on 6.1.1 only; on other builds the tests assert relations, which have not been run on 8.1.2 yet.

Limits: one synthetic 1000 Hz tone and one train of single-sample clicks, 48000 Hz mono, ffmpeg 6.1.1. A single-sample click is the hardest case for atempo, so 122 from 40 is a worst case, not what speech or music gets. The refusal limits and every count are 6.1.1 numbers. Not tested: speech or music, other sample rates, the tempo option of other filters (rubberband), other builds, players.
