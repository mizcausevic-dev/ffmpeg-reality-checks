# FF65 distribution kit: FFmpeg apad: a 3 s Song Becomes a 12 s File Until You Set a Length

All numbers below come from the synthetic fixture in `episodes/ff65/fixture/` (experiment.mjs, run.mjs) and its tests, measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1; the tests also pass on 8.1.2, where the aac file under apad with -shortest can end up to about 230 ms longer than the picture and the amount varies from run to run.

### Description

You pad a short song with -af apad and the file never finishes. What stops it? We build a flat synthetic picture of 5 s (125 frames at 25 fps) and a 440 Hz tone of 3 s (144000 samples at 48000 Hz), pad the tone with apad in different ways and measure how long the picture and the audio come out. Every command carries a hard -t, so nothing can run on.

Inputs (lavfi): color=c=0x303030:s=96x72:r=25:d=5 and sine=frequency=440:sample_rate=48000:duration=3 (also a 7 s tone of 336000 samples, a 4.3 s picture, a looped still image). Video is libx264, audio pcm_s16le in mkv or aac in mp4.

Readers: ffprobe (video packet count, the end of the picture and of the audio from packet times, the format duration) and a decode read in Node (framemd5 line count for decoded frames, raw s16le sample count for decoded audio). The packet count equals the decoded frame count in all 26 outputs.

Control: no apad. The file is 5.000 s, the picture 125 frames, the audio 144000 samples (3.000 s). With -shortest and no apad: 75 frames, 3.000 s.

apad alone has no stop: the file is exactly as long as the -t guard. -t 8 gives 8.000 s and 384000 samples, -t 12 gives 12.000 s and 576000 samples, in aac too (8.000 s). The picture stays at 125 frames (5.000 s).

-shortest ends the file with the picture: 5.000 s, 125 frames at 25 fps, 150 at 30 fps, 5 at 1 fps. The audio comes up a little short: 238208 samples in pcm instead of 240000 (1792 samples, 37 ms), the same at every frame rate; 239616 in aac.

A length stops it exactly: apad=whole_dur=5, apad=pad_dur=2, apad=pad_len=96000 and apad=whole_len=240000 all give 240000 samples (5.000 s). whole_dur sets the audio length, not the file length: whole_dur=4 gives 192000 samples (4.000 s) under the 5.000 s picture. apad never cuts: a 7 s song with whole_dur=5 stays 336000 samples. In aac, whole_dur=5 decodes to 240640 samples (235 x 1024).

Catches: a looped still image is endless, so apad with -shortest runs to the guard: 8.000 s, 200 frames, 384000 samples. apad=whole_dur=5 with -shortest ends it at 5.000 s (125 frames, 240000 samples). A picture of 4.3 s at 25 fps is 108 frames (4.32 s); whole_dur=4.3 and pad_dur=1.3 give 206400 samples (4.300 s), 0.02 s shorter than the picture.

The 15 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2. The exact counts are asserted on 6.1.1 only; on 8.1.2 the tests assert the relations.

Limits: one synthetic flat picture, sine songs of 3 s and 7 s, libx264, pcm in mkv and aac in mp4, ffmpeg 6.1.1. How -shortest cuts the audio is build and muxer specific, so treat the 238208 and 239616 counts as 6.1.1 numbers. Not tested: other encoders (x265, hardware), other containers, long songs, variable frame rate, real music and footage, players, other audio filters that pad (aresample async, amix), builds other than 6.1.1.
