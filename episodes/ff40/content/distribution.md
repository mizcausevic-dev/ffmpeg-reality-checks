### Description

You crop with crop=w:h:x:y at an odd offset. Is the picture really where you asked, in yuv420p, where chroma is stored at half resolution? We measure it with coded test cards, so every pixel names its own column and row.

Source (lavfi, lossless FFV1, mkv): two 192x108 cards. On the first, luma is the column and chroma is its own column and row; on the second, luma is the row. Two readers: ffprobe (width, height, pix_fmt) and the decoded raw planes, read in Node (the first column and row kept, in luma and in chroma), plus the frame hash to compare one crop with another. Control: an even offset, crop=96:54:4:6, starts at column 4 row 6 with chroma aligned.

Default (exact=0): crop=96:54:3:5 asks for column 3 row 5 and the picture starts at column 2 row 4, the same frame as crop=96:54:2:4. crop=96:54:1:1 starts at 0,0 and is the same frame as 0:0. An odd size is rounded down too: crop=95:53:3:5 is 94x52. No warning, and chroma stays aligned with luma.

exact=1: luma starts at column 3 row 5 as asked and the size stays odd (95x53), but chroma starts at chroma column 1 row 2, so the color is one luma pixel off (half a chroma sample). Control in yuv444p: offset 3,5 is honored with or without exact=1, and 95x53 is kept. In yuv422p only x moves (column 2, row 5 kept).

Centered crop: crop=90:50 in the 192x108 frame asks for x=51 y=29 and starts at column 50 row 28: margins 50 and 52 columns, 28 and 30 rows. With exact=1 the margins are 51 and 51, 29 and 29.

The twelve tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
