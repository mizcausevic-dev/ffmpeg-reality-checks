# FS07 distribution kit: FFmpeg AV1: -crf 0 Is Lossless in libaom, Not in libsvtav1

All numbers below come from the synthetic fixture in `episodes/fl07/fixture/` (experiment.mjs, run.mjs) and its tests, measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1 (Ubuntu build, SVT-AV1 1.7, libaom 3.8.2) and on ffmpeg 8.1.2 (Windows build, SVT-AV1 4.1); the two evidence files are `evidence-6.1.1.json` and `evidence-8.1.2.json`.

### Description

You type -crf 0 for lossless. libaom-av1 gives you a lossless file; libsvtav1 reads 0 as not set and uses CRF 35. Measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2, in under three minutes.

libaom: 236102 B on 6.1.1, 233918 B on 8.1.2, SSIM 1, PSNR infinite, decoded frames equal the source. libsvtav1: the same packets as no -crf at all, the encoder log says CRF / 35, 49746 B (SSIM 0.9946) on 6.1.1 and 60254 B (SSIM 0.9885) on 8.1.2.

Also in this short: the same -crf 30 writes 61302 B (6.1.1) and 82283 B (8.1.2) in libsvtav1 and 41459 B and 41340 B in libaom; -b:v 0 changes nothing; -b:v 500k is obeyed by libaom (511644 B to 223154 B on 8.1.2) but ignored by SVT-AV1 1.7 and refused by SVT-AV1 4.1; preset 12 is 55% bigger than preset 8; libsvtav1 puts keyframes at 0, 161, 322, libaom only at 0, and a seek to frame 300 runs 140 or 301 frames, or 1 with -g 25.

The full film (about six minutes) is: FFmpeg AV1: -crf 0, -b:v and Presets in libsvtav1 vs libaom.
