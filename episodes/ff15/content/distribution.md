### Description

You add a soundtrack to a video and the lengths do not match. FFmpeg has -shortest for that. What does it cut, and how exactly? We build a 5 second video (125 frames), a 3 second tone and an 8 second tone, and measure.

Readers: ffprobe (the durations the file declares) and a full decode (video frames counted, audio decoded to raw samples and measured).

No -shortest, 3 second tone: the file runs 5 s, as long as the video. The sound ends at 3 s; the last 2 seconds are silent. 125 frames.

-shortest with -c copy: the file ends at 3 s, but the picture stops early: 73 of 125 frames, 2.92 s. The picture ends 80 ms before the sound.

-shortest with a re-encode: both end together, 75 frames, 3 s.

8 second tone, no -shortest: the file runs 8 s but the picture ends at 5 s; 3 seconds of sound play over no picture. With -shortest the sound is trimmed to 5 s and all 125 frames are kept.

Same results on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
