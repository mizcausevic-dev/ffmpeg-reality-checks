### Description

You encode a screen recording with FFmpeg and thin red text looks fuzzy and a little purple. Is it the encoder? We draw one red line, one pixel wide, on blue, encode it three ways with no loss, and read it back.

Source: a 320x180 pure blue picture (0,0,255) with one red line (255,0,0), one pixel wide: a vertical line at x=100 and a horizontal line at y=90, made by lavfi. Encoded with libx264 -crf 0 (lossless in YUV, so only the chroma sampling can change a pixel) as yuv444p, yuv422p and yuv420p.

Readers: decode to rgb24 and read the pixels across the line, and ffmpeg signalstats on a 1x1 crop at the line (the U and V chroma values).

Vertical line: yuv444p keeps pure red (254,0,0), neighbors pure blue. yuv422p and yuv420p turn the line purple (150,21,149), and the pixel beside it becomes (104,0,103). The line's chroma moves from U 90, V 240 to U 175, V 167.

Horizontal line: yuv422p only halves chroma across, so the line stays red (253,0,0). yuv420p also halves it down: the line becomes (137,24,169) and the rows above and below are tinted.

Brightness (Y) at the line stays near 81 in all six files: color is lost, not light.

The six tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
