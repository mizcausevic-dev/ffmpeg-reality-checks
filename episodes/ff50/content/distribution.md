### Description

You downsample audio with aresample and a tone above the new Nyquist limit has nowhere to go but down. Does a 20 kHz tone fold into an audible 4 kHz one? We resample pure tones from 48000 Hz to 24000 Hz with ffmpeg 6.1.1 and read the level at the place where the alias would land, in dB against the input tone.

Source (lavfi aevalsrc, float wav, mono, 48000 Hz, 2 s): a pure sine at amplitude 0.25, which reads -15.1 dB RMS. Tones at 6000, 12100, 12500, 13000, 14000, 16000, 20000 and 23000 Hz. The new rate is 24000 Hz, so the new limit is 12000 Hz and a tone at f above it can only land at 24000 minus f.

Readers: (1) Node, a 32768 point Hann FFT on the decoded float samples, level of the band around the frequency; (2) ffmpeg itself, a bandpass filter 400 Hz wide around the frequency followed by the astats filter, RMS level. Where the alias is above -90 dB they agree within 1.5 dB. Below that the ffmpeg reader meets its own noise floor, and the Node reader is the more sensitive one.

Control: a 6000 Hz tone through aresample=24000 comes out at 6000 Hz with a level change of 0.0 dB in both readers. Known positive: the 20000 Hz tone decimated by hand in Node, every second sample and no filter, puts the full tone at 4000 Hz: 0.0 dB in both readers, so both can see an alias when there is one.

Results with the default aresample=24000: the 20000 Hz tone leaves an alias at 4000 Hz of -106.1 dB (Node) and -103.0 dB (ffmpeg reader). Tones at 14000, 16000, 20000 and 23000 Hz are all 90 dB down or better. Close to the limit it is different: a 12100 Hz tone comes back at 11900 Hz at -11.8 dB (Node) and -12.2 dB (ffmpeg reader); 12500 Hz leaves -19.4 dB at 11500 Hz; 13000 Hz leaves -34.0 dB at 11000 Hz.

The filter does the work: aresample=24000:filter_size=N on the 20000 Hz tone gives an alias of -7.0 dB (N=1), -15.5 (2), -30.7 (4), -88.3 (8), -91.2 (16) and -106.1 dB (32, the default). -ar 24000, aresample=24000 and aformat=sample_rates=24000 give the same samples (same hash).

Measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1; 8.1.2 pending. The 14 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1.
