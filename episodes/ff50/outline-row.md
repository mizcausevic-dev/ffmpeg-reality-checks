| 50 | Downsampling with `aresample`: does a high tone alias into the audible range? | Pure sine (lavfi `aevalsrc`, amplitude 0.25, 48000 Hz, mono, 2 s, float wav) resampled to 24000 Hz, so the new limit is 12000 Hz and a tone at f above it can only land at 24000 minus f. Readers: Node 32768 point Hann FFT on the decoded samples, and ffmpeg `bandpass` (400 Hz) + `astats` RMS level; they agree within 1.5 dB wherever the alias is above -90 dB. Control: a 6000 Hz tone comes out at 6000 Hz, 0.0 dB change. Known positive: the 20000 Hz tone decimated by hand in Node (every second sample, no filter) gives the full tone at 4000 Hz, 0.0 dB in both readers. Default `aresample=24000`: the 20000 Hz tone leaves -106.1 dB (Node) / -103.0 dB (ffmpeg) at 4000 Hz; 14000, 16000, 20000, 23000 Hz tones are all 90 dB down or better. Near the limit it comes back: 12100 Hz gives -11.8 / -12.2 dB at 11900 Hz, 12500 Hz -19.4 dB, 13000 Hz -34.0 dB. `filter_size` 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 (default) on the 20000 Hz tone: -7.0, -15.5, -30.7, -88.3, -91.2, -106.1 dB. `-ar 24000`, `aresample=24000` and `aformat=sample_rates=24000` give identical samples | Measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1 (8.1.2 pending).
