# FL07 distribution kit: FFmpeg AV1: -crf 0, -b:v and Presets in libsvtav1 vs libaom

### Description

Is -crf the same in libsvtav1 and libaom-av1? What does -crf 0 do, does -crf need -b:v 0, and what do presets and keyframes cost? Measured on synthetic clips with ffmpeg 6.1.1 (SVT-AV1 1.7, libaom 3.8.2) and ffmpeg 8.1.2 (SVT-AV1 4.1).

Short cut of this film (vertical, under 3 minutes): FFmpeg AV1: -crf 0 Is Lossless in libaom, Not in libsvtav1.

-crf 0: libaom is lossless: 236102 B on 6.1.1 (233918 B on 8.1.2), decoded frames equal the source, SSIM 1, PSNR infinite, packet for packet the same as lossless=1. libsvtav1 reads 0 as not set: the same packets as no -crf, and the encoder log says CRF / 35. 49746 B and SSIM 0.9946 on 6.1.1, 60254 B and SSIM 0.9885 on 8.1.2. -crf 64 fails in both ("out of range [0 - 63]", [-1 - 63] for libaom), -crf 63 works.

The same -crf 30 on the clean clip: libsvtav1 preset 8 writes 61302 B on 6.1.1 and 82283 B on 8.1.2, libaom 41459 B and 41340 B.

-b:v: next to -crf 30, -b:v 0 changes nothing (identical packets, both encoders, both builds). A real -b:v 500k: libaom obeys it (noisy clip 515938 B to 201811 B on 6.1.1, 511644 B to 223154 B on 8.1.2, SSIM 0.7286 and 0.7503). libsvtav1 1.7 on 6.1.1 ignores it (the same 61302 B file). libsvtav1 4.1 on 8.1.2 refuses it: "Target Bitrate only supported when --rc is 1/2 (VBR/CBR). Current --rc: 0", and no file is written.

Presets (libsvtav1, -crf 30, clean clip): preset 4, 8, 12 = 45764, 61302, 95105 B on 6.1.1 and 55893, 82283, 129910 B on 8.1.2. Preset 12 is 55% and 58% bigger than preset 8, with lower SSIM (0.9904 against 0.9967, and 0.9833 against 0.9919). On the noisy clip: 621923 B to 729503 B, and 708469 B to 746977 B.

Keyframes (400 frames, 25 fps): libsvtav1 at frames 0, 161, 322 (0, 6.44, 12.88 s), libaom at frame 0 only, -g 25 gives 16 in both. Seeking to frame 300 runs 140 frames (libsvtav1), 301 (libaom), 1 with -g 25. -g 25 costs libaom 18% (145674 B to 172376 B) on 6.1.1 and 19% on 8.1.2, libsvtav1 1% and 5%.
