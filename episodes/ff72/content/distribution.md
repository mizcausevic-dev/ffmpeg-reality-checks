### Description

You encode FLAC with -compression_level from 0 to 12. Is the file smaller, and is the sound the same? We measure four synthetic signals at all 13 levels with the native flac encoder.

Signals (lavfi, 3 s, 44100 Hz, stereo, 16 bit, 529200 bytes of raw PCM each): white noise (fixed seed), two pure tones per channel, a decaying music like chord with a little noise, and silence. Each is written once as a wav and then encoded at levels 0 to 12: 52 files.

Readers: (1) size and frames: Node reads the file size, and parses the FLAC bytes itself (STREAMINFO, then every frame header, each checked with its CRC-8 and its frame number); ffprobe gives the file size and lists the packets. Frame count and every frame size agree in all 52 files. (2) sound: three md5 sums of the decoded samples: ffmpeg's md5 muxer, Node on the raw s16le that ffmpeg decodes, and the md5 the encoder stored in the file's STREAMINFO (taken from the encoder input, independent of the decoder).

Control and known positives: no -compression_level gives exactly the same file as level 5. The same tones shifted by one sample give a different md5 in all three readers. Levels 13 and 99 are rejected ("invalid compression level") and no file is written.

Same sound: all 52 files decode to the md5 of their source (4 different sounds, 4 sums).

Smaller, from level 0 to 12: tones 183488 to 95377 bytes (-48.0%), music 232226 to 204463 (-12.0%), noise 277543 to 255844 (-7.8%), silence 9900 to 8696 (-12.2%). From the default level 5 to 12: tones 108103 to 95377 (-11.8%), music 209382 to 204463 (-2.3%), noise 255844 to 255844 (0%).

Not a straight line: noise at level 6 is 255971 bytes, 127 more than level 5 (255844); level 8 is 256016, bigger than level 7; music at level 10 is 5 bytes bigger than level 9; tones levels 6 and 7 are the same size. The big step is between level 2 and 3: the block size goes from 1152 to 4608 samples, so the frames go from 115 to 29, and tones drop from 139352 to 118204 bytes (-15.2%).

Header: every file starts with 8288 bytes (STREAMINFO, a 46 byte comment, 8192 bytes of padding from the muxer). Silence at level 3 and up is 8696 bytes: the header plus 408 bytes of audio.

The 11 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
