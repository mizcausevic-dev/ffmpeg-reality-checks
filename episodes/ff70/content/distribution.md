# FF70 distribution kit: ffmpeg rotate 45 Degrees: 15643 Pixels Cut, rotw and roth Keep Them

All numbers below come from the synthetic fixture in `episodes/ff70/fixture/` (experiment.mjs, run.mjs) and its tests, measured on ffmpeg 6.1.1; the tests also pass on 8.1.2.

### Description

You rotate a clip by 45 degrees with the rotate filter and the corners vanish. The filter keeps the input size by default, so the turned frame no longer fits. How much is cut, and how big must the output be? We measure it on a synthetic card.

Card (lavfi, 1 frame, rgb24): 320x240 mid gray (128, 128, 128, 76800 pixels) with four 24x24 corner markers (576 pixels each): red top left, lime top right, blue bottom left, yellow bottom right. Rotated by 15, 30, 45, 60 and 90 degrees at three sizes: the default (iw x ih), ow=rotw(a):oh=roth(a), and a 600x600 canvas (nothing can be cut: the reference). Two sampling modes: bilinear=0 (exact colors) and the default bilinear=1.

Readers: (1) Node reads the decoded rgb24 pixels: the pixels that are not the fill color, their bounding box, and the pixels within 40 of each marker color. (2) ffmpeg: ffprobe reads the output size, and signalstats on a plain gray card gives YAVG; YAVG / 128 x width x height is the surviving area. The two agree within 2 pixels in every run.

Control and known positives: rotate by 0 keeps 76800 pixels and 576 pixels per marker in both readers. Padding the card with 40 and 30 pixels of black adds exactly 43200 black pixels (both readers see it); a crop of 30 pixels on each side removes all four markers.

Default size: at 45 degrees all four markers are gone and 15643 card pixels are cut (62162 non fill pixels left, 77805 in the 600x600 reference), about a fifth. At 15 and 60 degrees two markers are gone and the other two are mostly cut; at 30 and 90 degrees all four are gone. The output stays 320x240.

rotw and roth: the rotated bounding box of the card, rounded: 371x315 (15), 397x368 (30), 396x396 (45), 368x397 (60), 240x320 (90). All four markers are kept at every angle. Only 7 to 11 tip pixels are clipped (7 at 45 degrees: a sweep of square canvases from 388 to 400 loses 111 pixels at 388, 7 at 396, 1 at 398 and 0 at 399 and 400, because of the one pixel sampling border).

Odd sizes: at 30 degrees rotw gives 397x368. libx264 with yuv420p refuses it: "width not divisible by 2 (397x368)". ow=ceil(rotw(a)/2)*2 and oh=ceil(roth(a)/2)*2 gives 398x368 and encodes, markers kept.

fillcolor: white fills 14638 pixels (the fill count, 76800 minus the 62162 card pixels left), and 0xff8000 gives (255, 128, 0). With format=rgba and fillcolor=none the corner is (0, 0, 0, 0), alpha 0; the rgba default is opaque black (0, 0, 0, 255); in yuv420p, fillcolor=none gives (0, 135, 0): green.

The 17 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
