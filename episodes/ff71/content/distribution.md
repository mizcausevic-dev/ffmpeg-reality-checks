### Description

You lift a dark clip with eq=brightness=0.1. How many gray levels does that add, and where does it clip? We measure the mapping level by level on synthetic input.

Source (lavfi, uncompressed rawvideo in mkv, yuv420p, chroma 128): 256 flat frames of 16x16 where frame n has luma Y = n (0 to 255), one file tagged limited range (tv) and one tagged full range (pc); and one 256x16 ramp frame where column x has luma x. Because every frame holds one level, the output level of a frame is the mapping of the filter at that level.

Readers: (1) Node reads the decoded Y plane (the first luma byte of every frame, and the 256 bytes of the ramp row). (2) ffmpeg signalstats gives YAVG, YMIN and YMAX per frame. They agree on all 256 levels for every filter tested, and on the ramp (mean 151.23, minimum 25, maximum 255).

Control and known positive: with no filter every level maps to itself in both readers and the range tags read back as tv and pc. lutyuv=y=clip(val+25,0,255) is a shift we wrote ourselves: both readers see plus 25 and the clip at level 230.

eq=brightness=0.1: level 0 becomes 25, level 100 becomes 125, 128 becomes 153; levels 230 to 255 (26 levels) all become 255. It is identical to lutyuv val+25 clipped at 255, level for level. Chroma stays 128. The steps are uneven: the shift is 2 at 0.02, 12 at 0.05 and 0.06, 22 at 0.09, 25 at 0.10 and 0.11, 27 at 0.12, 50 at 0.2, 76 at 0.3, and brightness 0.01 makes every level 1 darker. The same values come out with SIMD switched off (-cpuflags 0).

Range: the limited tagged and the full tagged file give the identical mapping, so eq ignores the range. On limited range video legal black 16 becomes 41 (the shift is 11.4 percent of the 219 level legal span, not 10), legal white 235 becomes 255, 25 legal levels end above 235 and 6 legal levels (230 to 235) merge into 255.

Contrast and gamma are range blind too: eq=contrast=1.5 sends levels 0 to 43 to 0 (legal black 16 included) and reaches 255 from level 214; eq=gamma=2.2 lifts legal black 16 to 72. A limited range aware lift, lutyuv=y=clip(val+22,16,235), ends at 235 (input 213 and up all give 235), so it still merges the top levels.

The 14 tests pass on ffmpeg 6.1.1 and 8.1.2.
